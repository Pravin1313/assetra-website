Umbrella Up – Help

How to play

Hold anywhere to open the umbrella. Warm rising air lifts you; still air lets you float down slowly. Drag left or right while holding to steer, or to walk along a ledge. Let go to close the umbrella and drop onto the ledge below.

Modes

Chill – no way to lose; rooftops every 50 m catch you and save your climb.
Storm – slippery ledges, strong gusts and no safety net. If your umbrella shakes in the wind, let go before it flips.
Race – beat three rivals to the 100 m rooftop on today's tower. Water balloons and banana peels are thrown for you automatically.

Saving clips and photos

Tap Clip or Photo, then Share, and choose "Save Video", "Save Image" or any app.

My progress disappeared

Progress is saved on your phone only. It is lost if the app is deleted or its data is cleared.
