Look Closer: Find & Rescue

Privacy Policy

Effective 1 November 2026 · Published by Pravin Purohit, Ahmedabad, India

In short: Look Closer does not ask for or store any personal information. There are no accounts and nothing to buy. The game shows ads through Google AdMob, which may use your device's advertising ID (you can ask for non-personalised ads or turn it off). Your game progress is saved only on your own phone.

Who we are

Look Closer ("the game") is made and published by Pravin Purohit, an independent developer based in Ahmedabad, Gujarat, India ("we", "us"). This policy explains what happens to information when you play the game on iPhone or Android.

Information we collect

We do not ask for your name, email, phone number, location, contacts, photos or any other personal information, and the game does not create an account. The only data collected is by our ad partner, Google AdMob, to show and measure ads: your device's advertising ID, IP address, device and app details, and how you interact with ads. See “Advertising” below.

What stays on your phone

To remember your progress, the game saves a small file on your device. It contains the levels you've finished, your stars and your best times. This data:

  • is never sent to us or to anyone else (your phone's own backup to iCloud or Google may include it, as it does for any app);
  • is deleted when you uninstall the game.

Permissions

PermissionWhy
Internet (Android default)Included in every Android app built with our framework. Used to load ads from Google AdMob; the game sends nothing else.

The game asks for no other permissions. Sound plays through your phone's normal game audio and follows its silent switch and volume.

Advertising

Look Closer is free and is supported by ads from Google AdMob. You may see a short video ad if you choose to watch one to get a hint (the first hint is free), and a full-screen ad between levels every few levels. Ads never appear in the middle of a level.

To show and measure ads, Google and its partners may use your device’s advertising ID (Android Advertising ID or iOS IDFA), your IP address and information about your device and how you use ads. This may be used to personalise ads. You can choose:

  • in the EU, UK and Switzerland, the game asks for your consent on first launch and you can change your choice later in the game;
  • on iPhone, the game asks for permission to track before using the IDFA (Settings > Privacy & Security > Tracking to change it);
  • on Android, you can reset or delete your advertising ID or opt out of ad personalisation in Settings > Google > Ads.

Google’s use of data is described at policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites. We do not sell your data, and apart from Google AdMob the game has no other analytics or tracking SDKs. We receive no personal data from Google.

Children

Look Closer is a cartoon game with adult humour and is rated 16+. It is not directed at children under 13 (or the relevant age in your country), and we do not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your rights

We hold no personal data about you. Data used for ads is handled by Google; you can manage it through the choices above and at myaccount.google.com. You stay in full control of the progress file on your phone. This policy is written to meet the requirements of India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act 2023, the EU/UK GDPR, the US COPPA and CCPA, and the Apple App Store and Google Play policies.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we'll post the new version on this page and update the effective date.
