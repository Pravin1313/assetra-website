Look Closer: Find & Rescue

Help & support

Find the thing. Drag the thing. Laugh.

How to play

  1. Every level has people (and animals, and ghosts) with a silly problem. The bubble over their head shows what's wrong.
  2. Tap anything to hear what it is. Drag an item onto a person or object to use it.
  3. Some things need combining first, like lemon plus soda for nimbu soda. Some hide in drawers and fridges; look for the sparkle.
  4. Fix every problem to finish the level. Fewer hints means more stars.

Stuck on a level?

  • Tap the light bulb. A finger shows you the next move you can make. Hints are free.
  • If you can't drag, you can also tap an item, then tap where it should go.
  • The round arrow at the top restarts the level straight away.

Progress

Your progress is saved on your phone. Deleting the game deletes your progress.
