Inkplop

Help & support

Draw anything. Tap GO. Get the ball in the box.

How to play

  1. Draw lines anywhere on the page with your finger. They stay exactly where you draw them.
  2. Tap GO. The ball and your lines drop like real objects.
  3. Get the ball into the orange box. Use fewer lines for more stars.

Some worlds add tools: springs, fans, portals and glue. Tap a tool, then press and drag on the page to place and aim it. Windmills, gates and hammers keep moving, so in those levels tap GO at the right moment.

Stuck on a level?

  • Tap the light bulb for a hint. It shows a line (and any tools) that solves the level. Hints cost coins, which you earn by finishing levels.
  • Undo takes back your last line or tool; the orange button at the top restarts the level.
  • You can't draw inside rain clouds, erasers, whirlwinds or force fields, but the ball passes right through them.

Progress and settings

Your progress is saved on your phone. Music and sound can be switched off in the shop (the coin button). Reset progress in the shop starts the game over.
